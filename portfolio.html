About Me

Getting to know me

A quick look at who I am, what drives me, and where I'm headed as a developer.

I'm Suyog Talekar, a frontend web developer who enjoys turning ideas into fast, clean, and interactive interfaces. My focus is on writing code that is both visually polished and easy to maintain.

I started with the fundamentals — C and C++ — before moving into the web, and I'm currently deepening my skills in React to build more dynamic, component-driven applications.

I care about small details: a smooth transition, a well-aligned grid, an interface that just feels right. That attention to detail is what I bring to every project.
